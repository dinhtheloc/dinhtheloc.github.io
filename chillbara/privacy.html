Privacy Policy

Chillbara: Study & Focus Timer · Effective 15 November 2026

Chillbara is a focus timer for iPhone, published by Loc Dinh ("we"). This policy explains what the app keeps on your device, what it sends, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Chillbara has no account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Everything you make in the app stays on your iPhone.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to help us improve it. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.
  • There are no ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your soaks, tower photos, guestbook, friendship levels, letters, yuzu, decor and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us. They may be included in your own iPhone backup, which Apple handles under its own terms.

What the app sends when "Share usage data" is on

Usage statistics, through Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC):

  • events about how the app is used, such as a soak starting or ending and its length, which in-app visitor or item was involved (by its internal content name), which screen was opened, and which setting was changed;
  • a random app-instance identifier created by the app, so that events from one install can be counted together;
  • general device information such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate country.

Crash and error reports, through Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.):

  • what the app was doing when it crashed, froze or hit an error, including a stack trace and the recent in-app steps that led to it;
  • device model, iOS version, app version, and free memory and storage at the time;
  • an error message, which may occasionally include a file path inside the app's own storage.

We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location, anything you type, or the advertising identifier. The device's vendor identifier is not collected, ad personalisation is off, and Sentry is set not to store IP addresses. None of this data is linked to your identity, and none of it is used for advertising or sold.

Your choices

  • Turn it off: Settings → About → Share usage data. While it is off, the app sends nothing to Firebase or Sentry.
  • Reset the identifier: Settings → About → Erase all data removes your data from the device and resets the analytics identifier.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all data it stored on your iPhone.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): for reminders when a soak or a break ends. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Photos, add only (optional): only when you choose to save a tower photo to your library. The app cannot read your photos.
  • Motion: when Flip to start is on, the app reads the phone's orientation on the device to know it is face down. This is not stored or sent.

How long data is kept

Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. Crash and error reports are kept by Sentry for 90 days or less. Data on your device stays until you erase it or delete the app.

Where data is processed

Firebase and Sentry process data on servers in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their own policies apply to that processing: Google Privacy Policy and Sentry Privacy Policy.

Children

Chillbara does not ask for personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app starts collecting something new, the page will say so before that version is released.
