Chillbara Support

Help for Chillbara: Study & Focus Timer on iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to locdtptit@gmail.com. Telling us your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version at the bottom of Settings helps us answer faster.

Common questions

Why did my tower topple?

A soak asks you to stay off other apps. If you leave Chillbara for more than about 10 seconds, the tower falls and the soak ends. Locking the screen is fine, and so is a short glance at a notification.

Can I lock my phone during a soak?

Yes. The soak keeps running with the screen locked. You can also turn on Flip to start in Settings and lay the phone face down to begin.

Can I pause?

Yes, when Allow pausing is on in Settings. Pause from the soak screen and carry on when you are back. Paused time does not count as focus time, and you still earn the full reward when the soak ends.

How do visitors work?

A visitor arrives every 10 minutes of focus, up to 8 in one soak. Who comes depends on the time of day, how long you soak, and what you have set out at the spring. The Guestbook gives a hint for each friend you have not met.

What is yuzu for?

You earn 1 yuzu for each minute of a finished soak. Spend it in Decorate on decor and treats. Some of them invite certain visitors, and treats last 3 soaks.

I am not getting reminders.

Turn on Break & tower alerts in Chillbara's Settings, then check that notifications are allowed for Chillbara in the iPhone's Settings app. A Focus mode or Do Not Disturb can also silence them.

I cannot hear the focus sound.

Turn on Focus sound in Settings. It plays while a soak is running, also with the screen locked, and stops during pauses and breaks. Check the iPhone's volume, and that no other app has taken over audio.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your soaks, tower photos, guestbook and decor are stored on your iPhone. Chillbara has no account and no cloud sync, so they travel only with an iPhone backup or a device-to-device transfer.

How do I start over?

Settings → About → Erase all data removes your tower photos, guestbook, yuzu, decor and settings from the device. This cannot be undone.

How do I turn off usage data?

Settings → About → Share usage data. When it is off, the app sends nothing. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected when it is on.
