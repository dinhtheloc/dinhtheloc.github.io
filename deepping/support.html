Help for Deep Ping: Idle Incremental on iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to locdtptit@gmail.com. Telling us your iPhone model, iOS version and the depth you are on helps us answer faster.

Common questions

How do I play?

Drag one finger to move your sonar ring. It pings on its own, and every creature inside the ring takes the hit. Move over what they drop to collect it, then spend it on upgrades in the hub and dive again.

Why does every dive end?

A dive ends when your air or your hull runs out. That is how the game works, even when the screen says "Dive Lost": you keep everything you recovered. Early dives are short; upgrades make them longer and richer.

How do I get to the next depth?

Survive long enough and the boss of the depth appears. Beat it and the next depth unlocks; the game moves you there straight away. To go back to an earlier depth, use the arrows next to DIVE in the hub. A depth you have already cleared is a free dive with no boss: press Surface when you want to leave.

What is free, and what does the full game unlock?

Depths 1 to 4 are free. After you beat the boss of Depth 4, a one-time purchase unlocks the full game: every depth and unlimited dives. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no other purchases.

I bought the full game but it is locked.

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. The same button is on the purchase screen. If it stays locked, write to us.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game keep going while it is closed?

The Refinery does. Feed it Scales and it turns them into Essence while the game is closed, for up to 8 hours. Dives only run while you play.

Where is my progress? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your progress is stored on your iPhone. Deep Ping has no account and no cloud sync, so it travels only with an iPhone backup or a device-to-device transfer. Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any iPhone.

How do I start over?

Settings → Erase Save removes all upgrades, resources and depths from the device. This cannot be undone. Your purchase is kept.

Can I watch the opening dive again?

Yes. Settings → Watch Intro replays it. Your progress is not touched.

How do I change sound, haptics or Game Center?

Settings has Music and Sound Effects volume, a Haptics switch, and a Game Center Achievements switch, which is off until you turn it on. If you hear nothing, also check the iPhone's volume and the silent switch.

How do I turn off usage data?

Settings → Share Anonymous Usage Data. When it is off, the game sends no statistics. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected when it is on.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
