Deep Ping

Privacy Policy

Deep Ping: Idle Incremental · Effective 16 November 2026

Deep Ping is a game for iPhone, published by Loc Dinh ("we"). This policy explains what the game keeps on your device, what it sends, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Deep Ping has no account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone.
  • The game sends anonymous usage statistics to help us improve it. You can turn this off in the game's Settings at any time.
  • There are no ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your save (resources, upgrades, depths reached, milestones, refinery and lighthouse progress) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us. They may be included in your own iPhone backup, which Apple handles under its own terms.

What the game sends when "Share Anonymous Usage Data" is on

The game sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC), so that we can see how the game is played and improve it:

  • milestones in the game, such as the opening dive, a dive starting or ending and how it ended, a depth being cleared, an upgrade being bought, a feature being unlocked, the full-game screen being shown or closed, and the save being erased;
  • when the app is opened and how long a session lasts;
  • in-app purchases: that the full-game purchase was started, and whether it completed or a restore succeeded;
  • a random app-instance identifier created for this install, so that events from one install can be counted together;
  • general device information such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate region. Google derives the region from a masked IP address; the game does not use your phone's location services.

This data is tied to that random identifier, not to your name, an account or Game Center. The game does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the vendor identifier (IDFV). The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only learns from Apple whether the full game has been bought on your Apple Account.

Game Center

Game Center achievements are optional and off by default. If you turn on Settings → Game Center Achievements, the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile. Apple operates Game Center under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Turn usage data off: open Settings in the game and switch off Share Anonymous Usage Data. The game stops sending statistics immediately, and it stays off the next time you open the app.
  • Erase your progress: Settings → Erase Save removes your progress from the device. Your purchase is kept by Apple and can be restored.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all data it stored on your iPhone.

Permissions

Deep Ping does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications, and it does not ask for permission to track you.

How long data is kept

Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. Data on your device stays until you erase it or delete the app.

Where data is processed

Google processes usage statistics on servers in the United States and other countries where it operates. Google's own terms apply to that processing: Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Children

Deep Ping does not ask for personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the game starts collecting something new, the page will say so before that version is released.
